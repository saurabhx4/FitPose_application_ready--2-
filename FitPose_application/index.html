✦ About FitPose

Movement science, made usable.

What FitPose is, what informs it, and where it's headed next.

What is FitPose?

FitPose is an AI movement coach that watches your form through your own camera and turns real-time posture cues into simple, spoken and visual feedback — built to make consistent movement feel achievable, not clinical.

Why physiotherapy matters

Small, correctable movement patterns — a rounded lower back, an uneven squat, tight hips from sitting — compound over time. Guided, consistent movement work is one of the most accessible ways to build and protect long-term mobility.

Our research foundation

FitPose's pose-detection and feedback logic draws on established areas of study: biomechanics and movement quality, computer-vision pose estimation, safety-first feedback prioritization, and behavior design for habit formation.

Where we're headed

Planned enhancements include exercise-specific explainers, condition-aware recovery plans built with licensed physiotherapists, deeper multilingual voice coaching, and clearer long-term progress analytics.

A note on scope

FitPose provides fitness and movement guidance. It is not a medical diagnostic tool and does not replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional or physiotherapist where one is needed.

✦ Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Everything people usually ask before their first FitPose session.

Is FitPose a substitute for a physiotherapist?

No. FitPose gives fitness and movement guidance, not a diagnosis. For pain, injury or a medical condition, please see a qualified healthcare professional.

How does the AI know if my form is correct?

Your camera's video is analyzed on-device with pose detection that tracks your joints in real time and compares your movement to the pattern expected for that exercise.

Does FitPose store or share my camera footage?

Pose analysis runs locally in your session; FitPose is built around clear, privacy-first controls rather than uploading raw video.

What languages does FitPose support?

The interface and AI Coach support English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati, Malayalam, Punjabi and Urdu — pick yours from the profile page.

Do I need any equipment?

No — every exercise in the library is bodyweight-only. You just need enough space to see your full body in the camera frame.

Can I change my weekly schedule?

Yes. Visit the Schedule page any time to swap a day's suggested exercise or regenerate the whole week from your AI Coach.
